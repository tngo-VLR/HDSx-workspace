# Provenance and Stewardship

**Data source:** The analysis uses the prepared NHANES Health Equity classroom CSV snapshot, `examples/nhanes-equity/data/nhanes_equity_v6.csv`, in this repository.

**Source of source:** The prepared dataset is derived from public-use National Health and Nutrition Examination Survey data released by CDC/NCHS.

**Access method:** The committed CSV is a cached course snapshot, loaded by a repository-relative path for offline, reproducible classroom work rather than retrieved afresh during analysis.

**Classroom-use boundary:** This prepared dataset supports descriptive learning activities and does not by itself support survey-weighted national estimates or causal conclusions.

**Privacy and stewardship caution:** Do not upload row-level records to external AI tools or use the classroom file to make individual-level claims.
